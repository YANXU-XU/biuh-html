Photography Projects in Progress

Small tools for the practical parts of photography, made to leave more room for looking.

Light Meter Pro
Compare a scene's light with an incident and reflected meter, then translate the reading into a starting exposure.
Photo Filter Lab
Preview restrained color and tone adjustments side by side without losing the character of the original frame.
Exposure Log Book
Keep aperture, shutter speed, focal length, and a short note together with each image.
Portrait Assistant
Use simple prompts for window light, background, and conversation before making a portrait.
Film Stock Finder
Browse film stocks by format, speed, color response, and the look they give to daylight.

A notebook on light, photographs, and the moments between frames.
